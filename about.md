# Larp Buttons

Ever saw someone's pause menu and wondered what the hell that button does?
want to confuse your friends?

Adds a bunch of <cy>completely useless</c> buttons to the pause menu. Dont like a certain button?
Every one of them can be switched off in the mod's settings.

## Buttons
* <cg>Upside-Down Pulse Trigger</c>: far left. Plays a sound.
* <cg>ZucchiniV2 Checkbox</c>: far right. You can check it. It does nothing.
* <cg>Pause Center</c>: below the continue button. Clicking it exits the level.
* Many more to find for yourself! (mainly in the pause menu)

Suggest more useless buttons in my discord server!
