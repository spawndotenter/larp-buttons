# Larp Buttons
Adds a bunch of <cy>completely useless</c> buttons to the pause menu. Every one of them can be switched off in the mod's settings.

## Buttons
* <cg>Upside-Down Pulse Trigger</c>: far left. Plays the chest sound and toggles "reverse pulse mode".
* <cg>ZucchiniV2 Checkbox</c>: far right. You can check it. It does nothing.
* <cg>Pause Center</c>: below the continue button. Clicking it exits the level.

More buttons are coming later.
